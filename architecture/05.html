Architecture 05

Connecting Multi Paths into Residents' Heart

A stepped artist-housing community reconnecting topography, green space, privacy, and neighborhood interaction

Individual academic project. I independently completed the concept development, site analysis, architectural and urban design, drawings, diagrams, modeling, and final visualization.

Award
Outstanding Architectural Design Award, Top Rank in Class, 2021

The project responds to fragmented green space and limited community interaction in Huam-dong.

The existing slope and neighborhood scale were preserved and reorganized into three stepped levels. Expanded alleys and shared courtyards draw greenery from Namsan into the residential community.

The housing was designed for 20 artists requiring both privacy and social connection. Each unit includes an independent entrance, atelier, terrace, or private garden, while shared gardens and community spaces create gradual levels of interaction.

The project connects fragmented green space and social relationships while preserving the site's topography and fine-grained neighborhood scale.
