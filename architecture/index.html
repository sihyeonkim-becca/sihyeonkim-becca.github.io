Architecture

Six studio projects, one recurring question

My architectural education established the foundation of my current research. Through environmental simulation, adaptive reuse, urban regeneration, mobility planning, and performance-based façade design, I learned to understand architecture as a system that shapes human comfort and urban relationships. These projects led me from the scale of the individual building toward neighborhood- and city-scale environmental research.
