Architecture 03

Circulation of Communication: Mobility-Based Yongsan Renewal

A modular and mobility-based urban system responding to changing retail, work, and housing patterns

Group academic project.

Award
Encouragement Award, Korea Space Design Association National Competition, 2023

The project investigates how purpose-built vehicles and autonomous mobility could become architectural spaces rather than transportation devices alone.

Existing retail, logistics, and pedestrian circulation within Yongsan Electronics Market were reorganized through a multi-level system. Underground routes accommodated logistics and autonomous mobility, while the ground level became a pedestrian-oriented linear plaza.

Modular steel additions accommodated retail, office, residential, and community functions. PBV units could operate as mobile showrooms, service spaces, medical units, pop-up stores, and shared community facilities.

The project demonstrates mobility planning, modular construction, mixed-use architecture, and urban-scale circulation design.

By treating mobility as an adaptable spatial system, the project proposes a district capable of responding to rapid industrial and social change.
