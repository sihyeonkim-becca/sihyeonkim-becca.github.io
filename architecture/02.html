Architecture 02

Urban Play Hub: Yongsan Electronics Market Regeneration

AI-assisted urban regeneration for a declining electronics district

Individual academic project. I independently completed the concept development, site analysis, architectural and urban design, drawings, diagrams, modeling, and final visualization.

Yongsan Electronics Market once functioned as a major center for computer products, distribution, and retail. Changes in online commerce and the technology industry reduced visitor activity and left many spaces underused.

The project asks how future technology can be introduced without erasing existing merchants, employment, and local industrial identity.

AI tools were used during early massing exploration to generate alternative spatial relationships, loops, connective circulation, and accessible public structures. These images were interpreted rather than directly adopted, and were translated into a site-specific master plan.

The proposal reorganizes the district around productive play, healing play, technology production, education, community programs, and green circulation. Vehicle, pedestrian, and freight flows were separated and restructured at the urban scale.

AI was used as a design-thinking tool to expand spatial alternatives, while the final master plan was developed through architectural and urban judgment.
