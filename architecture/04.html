Architecture 04

Smart Farm + Skin: Eco-Friendly Office

A performance-based double-skin office integrating smart farms, terraces, daylight, and natural ventilation

Individual academic project. I independently completed the concept development, site analysis, architectural and urban design, drawings, diagrams, modeling, and final visualization.

Award
Award Winner, Korean Society of Basic Design & Art, 2023 · Exhibited at Sejong Center for the Performing Arts

The project began with the observation that dense urban intersections contain substantial movement but few places for rest.

An office building was designed as a layered environmental "skin." The exterior double skin functions as a protective envelope, terraces operate as breathable intermediate spaces, and smart farms support an internal environmental cycle.

IES VE was used to evaluate daylight and building-performance alternatives. Perforation dimensions, window openings, and louver configurations were modified and compared to improve daylight distribution, natural ventilation, glare control, and lighting-energy performance.

The project demonstrates an early transition from form-based architectural design toward measurable, simulation-based environmental design.

The façade was designed not only as an image, but as a tested environmental system supporting daylight, ventilation, energy performance, and occupant well-being.
