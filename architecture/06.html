Architecture 06

Soyo-yu 逍遙遊: Museum Remodeling and Structural Reuse

Reinterpreting Itami Jun through preserved structure, vertical voids, ramps, material, and light

Individual academic project. I independently completed the concept development, site analysis, architectural and urban design, drawings, diagrams, modeling, and final visualization.

The project remodels a historically layered building into a museum dedicated to architect Itami Jun.

The concept of Soyo-yu, meaning wandering freely beyond constraint, was translated into a spatial sequence of ramps, voids, changing viewpoints, and light.

The primary core and columns were preserved, while a secondary core was relocated to create a vertical void and sunken public space. Exhibition spaces were organized through metal, wood, and light to represent the transition between Itami Jun's Japanese and Korean architectural works.

Structural reuse and spatial subtraction transform the existing building into a continuous architectural journey shaped by movement, material, and light.
