Project Overview

The existing Gloria Center consisted of traditional stage-style halls on the second and third floors and a multipurpose hall on the first floor. However, its aging facilities, limited spatial flexibility, and church-oriented layout restricted its use by the wider community.

The renovation transformed the building into an open and flexible community facility. The first floor was redesigned as a publicly accessible book café with an expanded outdoor terrace, while the new glass façade created a more transparent connection between the building and its surroundings. The second and third floors were converted into adaptable spaces for sports, community events, worship services, youth activities, meetings, and informal gatherings.

My Role

During my seven-month internship at Design Som, I participated in both the design development and on-site construction stages of the renovation. Working under the lead designer, I produced CAD drawings and SketchUp models to support the development and construction implementation of the new façade.

I regularly visited the construction site, documented construction progress and site conditions, and prepared daily construction reports. This experience allowed me to follow the project from the existing-building survey and façade design development through construction coordination and completion.

Key Contributions

  • Produced CAD drawings for the existing building and renovation work.
  • Developed SketchUp models for the façade design.
  • Supported façade design development and construction coordination.
  • Documented on-site conditions and construction progress.
  • Prepared daily construction reports.
  • Observed the realization of the façade from design to completed construction.
