Lucid Dream (Run Away) — 2022

Lucid Dream (Run Away)

자각몽: 도망

Year
2022
Medium
Oil Painting / Mixed Media
Recognition
Award Winner, 2022 JoongAng Fine Arts Competition

Concept

This work was created from the experience of living within a restricted and controlled environment. In a reality shaped by isolation and the desire for liberation, imagination becomes a form of escape.

The work enters another spatial dimension through the idea of a lucid dream. Although the scene belongs to the unconscious, its strong visual clarity makes it appear more immediate and real than ordinary reality.

The act of "running away" does not simply represent avoidance. It reflects the mind's attempt to create another world when external conditions feel closed and immovable.

In a restricted and controlled reality, imagination becomes a path toward liberation. This work depicts an escape into another dimension of lucid dreaming. Although the scene emerges from the unconscious, its visual clarity makes the dream feel more real than waking life. The work explores how the mind constructs alternative spaces when reality becomes difficult to endure.

Key Statement

When reality becomes restrictive, imagination creates another dimension in which escape becomes possible.
