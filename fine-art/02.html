Minerva Owl in the City — 2023

Minerva Owl in the City

도시의 미네르바 부엉이

Year
2023
Medium
Oil Painting · Oil Pastel
Recognition
Award Winner, 2023 JoongAng Fine Arts Competition

Concept

The work is inspired by the idea that Minerva's owl spreads its wings only at dusk — understanding emerges only after events have unfolded and the light of day has begun to disappear.

Within the exhausted environment of urban life, the owl's eyes become moons that look through turmoil and uncertainty. Its feathers expand into waves containing buildings, houses, and whales.

The city is not depicted as a fixed physical object. It becomes part of a living creature whose feathers carry memories, shelters, and imagined forms of freedom.

Minerva's owl opens its wings only at dusk. In this work, the owl's eyes become moons that penetrate the turbulence of the world and the exhaustion of urban life. Buildings, houses, and whales drift within the waves of its feathers, transforming the city into a symbolic landscape of reflection, shelter, and imagination.

Key Statement

At dusk, the owl reveals the hidden emotions, conflicts, and fragile shelters of the city.
