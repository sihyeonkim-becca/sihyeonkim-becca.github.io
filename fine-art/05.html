One Drop City

Year
2026
Exhibition
The 26th Korean Painting Status Exhibition

Concept

One Drop City begins with a single drop of dawn dew falling from the edge of a lily petal.

The drop gives life to a fish crossing a distant sea. The movement of the fish creates ripples, and those ripples gradually flow into a dark and silent city.

The work presents recovery not as a sudden transformation, but as a small and continuous cycle. A single natural element can awaken life, carry energy across distance, and allow the city to breathe again.

At the edge of a lily petal, a single drop of dawn dew falls. The drop gives life to a fish crossing the distant sea, and the movement of its body creates ripples that travel toward a darkened city. As the small wave enters the urban landscape, the city begins to breathe again. Even in darkness, light spreads through water and sleeping lives awaken. The work imagines recovery as a cycle that begins with one small natural force and gradually restores life to the city.

Key Statement

A single drop can awaken life, and one small cycle of nature can allow an entire city to breathe again.
