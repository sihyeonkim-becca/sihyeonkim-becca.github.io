Fine Art

Restriction, dream, reflection, fragility, recovery

My fine-art practice explores the emotional conditions of urban life — restriction, isolation, recovery, imagination, and the fragile persistence of life. Through oil painting, oil pastel, and digital art, I transform cities, animals, water, dreams, and celestial imagery into symbolic landscapes. While my research examines cities through data and environmental performance, my paintings approach the city through emotion, memory, and perception.

Artist Statement

My paintings explore how individuals emotionally experience cities and changing environments. Restriction, exhaustion, isolation, fragility, and recovery repeatedly appear in my work through symbolic images such as owls, fish, water, glass, dreams, and urban buildings.

The city in my paintings is not simply a physical background. It is an emotional organism that can become exhausted, fragmented, awakened, or healed. Natural elements often enter these urban landscapes as small but transformative forces — a drop of water, a fish, a feather, or a beam of light can carry life into spaces that appear dark or controlled.

This artistic practice complements my architectural and urban research. While my research uses simulation, data, and optimization to improve environmental conditions, my paintings examine how those environments are perceived and emotionally lived.
