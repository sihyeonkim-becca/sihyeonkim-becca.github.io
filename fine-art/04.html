John's Planet — 2024

John's Planet

Year
2024
Recognition
Award Winner, 2024 JoongAng Fine Arts Competition

Concept

John's Planet represents the human heart as the center of a fragile glass aquarium. The transparent structure can easily break, yet it continues to hold life within it.

A fish living inside the aquarium symbolizes persistence. Although the surrounding world may feel cold and empty, the fish discovers a place in which it can survive.

The narrator casts a net into the red center of the heart, hoping to find and recover the seed of life hidden in the deepest and coldest place.

A fragile heart forms the center of a glass aquarium. Although it can easily shatter, it continues to protect the life within it. A fish finds its home in this vulnerable interior, becoming evidence that life can endure even in a cold and empty environment. Looking into the red depth of the heart, I cast a net like a prayer, hoping that the hidden seed of life will rise and bloom again.

Key Statement

Fragility does not mean the absence of life; even a breakable heart can become a place where life survives.
