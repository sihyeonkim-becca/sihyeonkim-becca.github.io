Awaken

“부우, 부우. 이제는 일어나야 할 시간이다.”

Medium
Digital Art
Exhibition
Digital Art Exhibition, Art Galleria, Insadong, Seoul

Concept

Awaken captures the instant before consciousness fully returns. The repeated sound, "Boo, boo," functions like a signal crossing the boundary between sleep and reality.

The work focuses on transition: from stillness to movement, unconsciousness to awareness, and isolation to renewed perception.

“Boo, boo. It is time to wake up.” This work captures the transitional moment between sleep and consciousness. A distant signal calls the mind back into reality, suggesting awakening not only as a physical act but also as the recovery of perception, movement, and life.

Key Statement

Awakening begins when a distant signal interrupts stillness and returns the mind to life.
