Research Identity

I am an urban planning and architecture researcher specializing in Urban Heat Island mitigation, pedestrian thermal comfort, microclimate simulation, and AI-aided spatial optimization. My research investigates not only whether heat mitigation strategies are effective, but also where they should be implemented, how their effects vary across urban contexts, and how limited public budgets can achieve the greatest environmental benefit.

My work integrates ENVI-met and SOLWEIG microclimate simulations, GIS and Python-based urban data analysis, machine learning, genetic algorithms, and life-cycle cost assessment. Through this approach, I aim to develop climate-responsive and economically feasible planning strategies that connect physical environmental performance with real-world urban decision-making.
